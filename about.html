About CodeYantra

Building practical technology from a small beginning.

CodeYantra Technologies is an Indian technology company focused on practical, accessible and reliable software for individuals and businesses.

Mission

Building practical, accessible and reliable technology that solves genuine problems for individuals and businesses.

Vision

CodeYantra aims to grow as an Indian technology company building innovative solutions across software, artificial intelligence, automation and connected business technology.
