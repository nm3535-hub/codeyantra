Founder Story

Neeraj Mishra

Founder & Developer, CodeYantra Technologies

A non-traditional path into technology became a strength through continuous learning and execution.

From Ayodhya to building software products

Neeraj Mishra's journey originates from Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh. His academic path was not the conventional Computer Science route. He completed a Bachelor of Arts from Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia Avadh University, with foundational education associated with the Punjab School Education Board.

His early technical background came through Hardware & Networking, supported by Microsoft Certified Professional learning. Over time, he moved deeper into software through continuous self-learning, practical experimentation and persistent execution.

The modern development journey behind CodeYantra includes software development, Git, version control, databases, cloud technologies, automation and AI-assisted development.

Founder Message

Technology should remain useful, honest and accessible.

CodeYantra represents years of learning, struggle and steady building. Coming from a lower-middle-class background, Neeraj understands financial limitations, practical constraints and the pressure of real-world decisions.

Family and children are an important motivation behind the work. The goal is not technology for show, but useful solutions that respect the trust people place in the company.

When someone chooses CodeYantra, they are not simply choosing software; they are placing trust in the people building it.

What CodeYantra stands for

Honesty, quality, responsibility and technology that helps people work better without unnecessary complexity.
