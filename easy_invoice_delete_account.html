Account Deletion

Delete Your Easy Invoice Account

Use this page to request deletion of your Easy Invoice account and associated cloud-synced business data.

What will be deleted

After we verify your request, we will delete or anonymize the Easy Invoice account data associated with your registered email address, including cloud-synced records where applicable.

  • Account profile and sign-in related records
  • Cloud-synced invoices, parties, products, purchases, stock, ledgers, payments, reports, and settings linked to the account
  • Backup and restore data stored for the account, where applicable

Some information may be retained for legal, tax, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate business record requirements. Deleting your account does not automatically delete files you already exported, printed, downloaded, or shared outside the App.
