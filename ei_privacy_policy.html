Privacy Policy

Easy Invoice — GST Billing App by CodeYantra Technologies

Last Updated: April 03, 2026

Your privacy is our top priority. Easy Invoice stores all your business data — invoices, ledgers, party details — 100% locally on your device. We do not upload, access, or share your data with any server or third party.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the Easy Invoice application ("App") developed and published by CodeYantra Technologies ("we", "our", "us"). By downloading and using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Easy Invoice is currently a Windows desktop application. A cloud-sync feature is planned for a future update, and this policy will be updated accordingly before that release.

2. Data We Collect

Currently, Easy Invoice collects no data from you. All information you enter into the App (invoices, customer details, products, GST numbers, ledgers) is stored exclusively on your local device storage.

  • No account creation required
  • No personal data sent to our servers
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs embedded
  • No internet connection required to use the App

3. Upcoming Cloud Sync Feature

We are building a Cloud Sync feature that will allow you to access your invoices across multiple devices including mobile. When this feature is released:

  • Cloud sync will be opt-in only — you choose whether to enable it
  • Your data will be encrypted before upload
  • We will update this Privacy Policy before the feature goes live
  • You will be notified in-app of the policy changes

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your data with any third parties. Since no data leaves your device at this time, there is nothing to share.

5. Data Security

Your business data is stored in local application storage on your Windows device. The security of this data depends on your device's security settings. We recommend:

  • Keeping your Windows OS updated
  • Using a password-protected user account
  • Taking regular backups of your App data

6. Children's Privacy

Easy Invoice is a business application intended for adults (18+) running businesses. We do not knowingly collect any information from minors.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, especially when new features like Cloud Sync are released. The latest version will always be available on this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
