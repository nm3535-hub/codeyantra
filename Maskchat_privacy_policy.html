1. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CodeYantra Technologies ("Service Provider", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information.

1.1 Information We Collect

  • Location Data: Collected only when using "Shake & Connect". Not stored historically.
  • Camera & Gallery: Accessed only for profile pictures or sending images in chat.
  • Sensor Data: Accelerometer data processed locally for the "Shake" gesture.
  • User Content: Messages and media processed securely through Google Firebase.
  • Identifiers: IP, Device ID, and Ad IDs for security and monetization.

1.2 How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide core messaging, maintain safety (banning abusive users), and display advertisements via Google AdMob.

1.3 Data Retention & Deletion

Messages and media are automatically deleted after 90 days.

Instant Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data instantly via Settings > Delete Account. This is irreversible and immediate.

2. Advertising

MaskChat is supported by Google AdMob. You can manage personalized ad preferences in your device settings. For EU/UK users, we comply with the IAB TCF framework.

3. Child Safety & Zero Tolerance

STRICT POLICY: Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) is strictly prohibited. Users must be 18+ years old.

We use automated moderation to block nudity. Any CSAM detected will be reported to NCMEC and law enforcement immediately.

4. Acceptable Use

Harassment, bullying, hate speech, illegal activities, and impersonation are strictly prohibited and will result in a permanent ban.
