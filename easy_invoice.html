Multi-Location Stock

Multiple Warehouses,
One Billing System

Your office and warehouse can be in different cities or states — no problem. When creating a bill, simply select the dispatch location and stock is deducted from that warehouse automatically. GST (IGST or CGST+SGST) is calculated based on the dispatch state, not your office location.

Payment Tracking

Vendor Payment
Tracked on Both Sides

When you make a direct payment to a vendor — whether via bank transfer or cash — the transaction is recorded on both sides simultaneously. The vendor's outstanding balance decreases, and your bank or cash balance decreases. One entry, two ledgers updated.

  • Payment From: select any bank account, cash, or UPI as the payment source
  • Payment To: vendor's outstanding balance reduces automatically on the same entry
  • Bank/cash balance updates in real time — no separate journal entry needed
  • Payment mode recorded — NEFT, RTGS, Cheque, Cash, and UPI all tracked separately
  • Invoice-wise settlement — link each payment to the specific invoice it settles
  • Full audit trail — every payment recorded with date, amount, and mode

GST Smart Engine

Auto Tax. Zero Confusion.

Our smart GST engine detects whether your buyer is in the same state or another state — and automatically applies the correct tax structure without you lifting a finger.

  • Different State → IGST Inter-state supply correctly billed as a single Integrated GST
